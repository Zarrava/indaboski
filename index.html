About

Engineer first, researcher second — always shipping.

I'm Jordan Reyes, an AI engineer with 6 years building ML systems that survive contact with real users. I started in classical ML, moved into deep learning, and now spend most of my time on LLM applications and computer vision in production.

I care about the unglamorous work: evaluation harnesses, latency budgets, cost per request, and the handoff between model and product. I write code that the next engineer can actually read, and I measure everything.

Outside of work I write about applied ML and tinker with small open-source tools.
